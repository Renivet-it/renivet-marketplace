## Notes on completeness

This register reflects what was found in this pass's three read-only inventories plus direct Linear queries. It does **not** claim to be exhaustive of every one of Renivet's ~170 Linear issues — pre-audit product/marketing issues (roughly REN-1 through REN-90, e.g. campaign launches, UI tweaks, brand onboarding) are real work but outside this program's Enhancement & Improvements scope (they're feature/marketing backlog, not audit-derived remediation or research-derived architecture work) and are intentionally not rows here.
