# REN-274 Specification

Connect the Corporate catalog to one canonical product/configuration identity and converge direct purchase and RFQ→quote→PO/payment into the controlled Corporate order model. Retire or redirect conflicting legacy entry paths after parity. The catalog must never silently replace a selected product or lose its SKU/configuration.

Acceptance: published admin products are selectable; direct and RFQ paths preserve product/SKU through the approved commercial snapshot, order, FO, documents, and settlement; buyer/admin views expose consistent state and next action; legacy routes redirect safely.
