# REN-270 Specification

Make Corporate documents and tax outputs consume authoritative commercial/FO snapshots, validated HSN/GST and place-of-supply facts, persisted legal/customer identity, and authoritative unique numbering. Material brand invoice discrepancies block settlement unless explicitly overridden; missing documents are not-issued/pending, never fabricated.

Acceptance: wrong GSTIN/HSN/total, missing classification, address-state mismatch, unauthorized downloads, and numbering races are handled fail-closed with auditable correction/reissue.
